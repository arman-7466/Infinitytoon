⚠️ Important Instructions for Users

Since this is a 100% free platform, we rely on third-party video servers to host our content. To ensure you have the best watching experience, please follow these two mandatory steps:

1. 🛡️ VPN is REQUIRED

Videos not playing? In many regions, the video servers might be blocked by your internet service provider (ISP).

Fix: You MUST use a VPN to stream the episodes. Just connect to any free VPN (like Cloudflare 1.1.1.1, ProtonVPN, or Turbo VPN) before hitting the play button.

2. 🛑 How to Block Annoying Ads

Because the video players are from third-party sources, they might show pop-up ads when you click on them. You can easily block them without installing any app!

For Android/iOS:

Go to your phone's Settings.

Search for Private DNS (or DNS).

Select "Designated Private DNS" or "Custom DNS".

Type in: family.adguard-dns.com

Save and enjoy an ad-free experience!
